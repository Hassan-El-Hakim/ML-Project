Adults                       | Integer     | Number of adults.                                                                        | BO and BL
Agent                        | Categorical | ID of the travel agency that made the booking.                                           | BO and BL

Babies                       | Integer     | Number of babies.                                                                        | BO and BL
BookingChanges               | Integer     | Number of changes made to booking (persons, dates, nights, room, meal) before check-in.   | BO and BL
Children                     | Integer     | Number of children (sum of both payable and non-payable children).                       | BO and BL
Company                      | Categorical | ID of the company/entity responsible for booking or paying. Anonymous code.             | BO and BL
Country                      | Categorical | Country of origin represented in ISO 3155-3:2013 format.                                 | BO, BL and NT
CustomerType                 | Categorical | Type: Contract (allotment), Group, Transient (independent), Transient-party (linked).    | BO and BL
DaysInWaitingList            | Integer     | Number of days on the waiting list before confirmation.                                  | BO

DistributionChannel          | Categorical | Booking distribution channel (TA = Travel Agents, TO = Tour Operators).                  | BO, BL and DC
IsCanceled                   | Categorical | Value indicating if the booking was canceled (1) or not (0).                             | BO
IsRepeatedGuest              | Categorical | Value indicating if the booking is from a repeated guest (1) or not (0).                 | BO, BL and C
LeadTime                     | Integer     | Number of days between booking entry date and arrival date.                              | BO and BL
MarketSegment                | Categorical | Market segment designation (TA = Travel Agents, TO = Tour Operators).                    | BO, BL and MS
Meal                         | Categorical | Package: Undefined/SC (no meal), BB (Bed & Breakfast), HB (Half board), FB (Full board).  | BO, BL and ML
PreviousBookingsNotCanceled  | Integer     | Number of previous bookings not cancelled prior to this one. (0 if no profile).          | BO and BL
PreviousCancellations        | Integer     | Number of previous bookings cancelled prior to this one. (0 if no profile).              | BO and BL
RequiredCardParkingSpaces    | Integer     | Number of car parking spaces required by the customer.                                   | BO and BL
ReservationStatus            | Categorical | Last status: Canceled, Check-Out, No-Show.                                               | BO
ReservationStatusDate        | Date        | Date at which the last status was set.                                                   | BO
ReservedRoomType             | Categorical | Code of room type reserved. Anonymous code.                                              | BO and BL
StaysInWeekendNights         | Integer     | Number of weekend nights (Sat or Sun) stayed or booked.                                  | BO and BL
StaysInWeekNights            | Integer     | Number of weeknights (Mon to Fri) stayed or booked.                                      | BO and BL
TotalOfSpecialRequests       | Integer     | Number of special requests made (e.g., twin bed, high floor).                            | BO and BL
